The bell doesn’t lie.

Ring Ready is a round timer built for the way athletes actually train: boxing, MMA, Muay Thai, kickboxing and cross-training circuits.

Sounds that mean business

Twelve sound packs across bells, buzzers, whistles and chimes. Cues ring through the silent switch and mix over your music, and traditional clap sticks warn you when ten seconds remain.

A clock you can read across the gym

A huge, high-contrast countdown in portrait or landscape. Pick a fixed color, or let Auto mode burn from green to red as the round drains. The screen stays awake for the whole session.

The round, drawn around your screen

A progress meter traces the edge of the display. Choose the animation that pushes you: Pulse, Comet or Ember.

  • 1–20 rounds, or infinite
  • Round, rest and prepare durations to the second
  • Haptic cues you can feel through gloves
  • Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  • Eight themes, dark and light
  • No accounts. No ads. No subscription.
