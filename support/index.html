Frequently asked questions

I can’t hear the bell.

Round cues play at your media volume, so turn the volume up with the side buttons while the app is open. They still ring with the silent switch on. Also check that no Bluetooth speaker or headphones are connected by accident, and try a louder sound pack such as Arena Horn or Gym Buzzer in Settings.

How do I change the bell, buzzer or whistle?

Open the Settings tab and pick a category (Bells, Buzzers, Whistles or Chimes) in the Sounds section. Tap the speaker button to preview a sound, and tap the row to select it.

How do I set the number of rounds and the timings?

In Settings, use the Rounds section to choose 1–20 rounds or infinite, and set the round, rest and prepare durations to the second.

Can I turn off the 10-second warning or haptics?

Yes. Both are toggles in the Feedback section of Settings.

How do I change the theme or the meter animation?

Use the Appearance section of Settings for light, dark or system mode and one of eight themes. The Meter Animation section lets you choose Pulse, Comet or Ember.

Does Ring Ready collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
