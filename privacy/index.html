Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

The short version: Ring Ready does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. Ring Ready works entirely on your device and does not connect to any server that we operate. We don’t ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your preferences (such as round and rest durations, sound pack, theme, meter style and feedback settings) and the state of a session in progress locally on your iPhone so they are there next time you open it. This data never leaves your device, and we can’t see it. Deleting the app removes it.

Live Activities

If you start a session, Ring Ready can show the current round on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. This information is generated and displayed on your device only.

Third parties

Ring Ready contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it does not use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

Children’s privacy

Because Ring Ready collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
